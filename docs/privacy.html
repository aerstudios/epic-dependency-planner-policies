Privacy notice

How the app uses Jira information, where it is held, and how to raise a privacy question.

Who is responsible

This draft notice describes the processing performed by Epic Dependency Planner, an internal Jira Cloud app operated by Aer Studios Limited (“AER”, “we”, “us”). Its purpose is to help authorised colleagues understand dependencies, ownership, progress and project budgets.

For questions about the app or this notice, contact devs@aerstudios.co.uk. This notice covers the app and this policy website; AER’s wider handling of employment, contractor and client information is outside its scope.

AER review

Confirm AER’s controller/processor responsibilities for the Jira data in scope and the relationship to any existing staff, contractor or client privacy notices.

Information the app uses

The app reads information from Jira that the signed-in user is already permitted to access:

  • Project and issue identifiers, keys, summaries, issue types, statuses, priorities, epic membership and dependency links.
  • Current assignee display names and Atlassian account identifiers.
  • Original estimates, logged and remaining time, Percentage complete, and the configured Dev Contingency field.
  • The site and project context needed to display and link to the correct Jira issues.

Issue summaries and other Jira content may contain personal or confidential information entered by Jira users. Assignee normalization retains account identifiers and display names; it discards email addresses, avatars and additional profile details returned within assignee responses. The app does not request issue descriptions, comments, attachments or individual work-log entries.

Why this information is used

The app uses this information to display dependencies, recommend an explainable order of work, show current ticket ownership, and calculate budget consumption, forecasts, recovered and overburnt hours. Assignee filters and summaries help users review work by its current owner.

Assignee summaries group all time recorded on the currently assigned tickets. They do not identify who performed or logged that work, and they do not calculate a person’s available staffing capacity. The app does not make assignment changes or take decisions about a person’s employment.

Proposed lawful basis — for AER to confirm

Legitimate interests in coordinating authorised project work, understanding delivery dependencies and managing project budgets. AER must confirm this basis and any required balancing assessment, including the effect of assignee reporting on staff and contractors, before adopting this notice. Where AER acts on another controller’s instructions, the applicable instructions and agreement must also be confirmed.

Access, hosting and recipients

The app is hosted using Atlassian Forge. Jira reads operate through the Forge bridge as the signed-in user, and analysis takes place in the user’s browser. Jira project permissions, issue-security settings and applicable app-access controls continue to apply.

The app has no separate application database, Forge issue-data storage, external analytics service or external data-processing endpoint configured. It does not send Jira ticket information to GitHub. Atlassian provides Jira and Forge hosting and may process platform, security and operational information under the applicable Atlassian agreements.

Users can choose to export CSV or Markdown reports. Those reports may include Jira ticket content and assignee names, and users can subsequently share them. They should be handled in accordance with AER’s access and confidentiality requirements.

AER review

Confirm the relevant Atlassian and GitHub supplier arrangements, processing locations and any international-transfer safeguards. This draft makes no promise that hosting or support processing is restricted to a particular country.

Storage and retention

Fetched issue data is held in browser memory for the app session and is replaced when a new snapshot is loaded. The app does not write issue data to its own persistent database. Source tickets and platform records remain subject to Jira, Atlassian and AER’s retention arrangements.

Display-filter preferences and budget-field configuration are stored in browser local storage. They remain until overwritten or the relevant browser site data is cleared. Issue content and assignee names are not written to that preference storage.

Downloaded reports remain wherever the user saves them; closing or uninstalling the app does not delete those files. Messages sent to the support address are held in AER’s email systems.

AER review

Confirm the applicable retention periods or criteria for source Jira records, exported reports, support enquiries and any supplier operational records. In-browser session behaviour is not a retention policy for those separate records.

This policy website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no custom analytics, forms, advertising, third-party fonts, scripts or app-managed browser storage. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in.

GitHub’s own handling of website requests is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement and its GitHub Pages documentation. Following external links or sending an email involves those separate services.

Questions and privacy rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, object to certain processing, or request portability. These rights are subject to the conditions and exemptions in the applicable law.

Contact devs@aerstudios.co.uk with a privacy enquiry or to ask who is responsible for a particular Jira record. AER should assess requests concerning its processing and direct requests to the relevant controller or supplier where appropriate. The app itself does not provide a facility to delete source Jira records or previously downloaded reports.

You may also raise a concern with the applicable data-protection regulator. In the UK, information about raising a concern is available from the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this notice

This document is a review draft and has no adopted effective date. An approved version should identify its effective date and any material changes to the app’s purposes, data use, recipients or retention arrangements. Questions about changes can be sent to the contact address above.
