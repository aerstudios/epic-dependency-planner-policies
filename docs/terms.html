Terms of use

The intended use of the app, your responsibilities, and the limits of its recommendations and forecasts.

Purpose and scope

These draft terms describe authorised internal use of Epic Dependency Planner, operated by Aer Studios Limited (“AER”). The app supports Jira dependency planning, ticket ownership review and budget tracking. It is not offered here as a public commercial service or a commitment to provide support to other organisations.

Access to these public policy pages does not grant access to the app or to Jira. Any authorised distribution must follow AER’s decision about eligible users and sites. These terms do not replace applicable Atlassian agreements, employment or contractor terms, or client confidentiality obligations.

Authorised use and confidentiality

Use the app only for work you are authorised to access and manage. Follow the applicable Jira permissions, AER’s information-handling requirements and any relevant client restrictions. Do not attempt to bypass access controls, misuse another person’s account or use the app to obtain data you are not permitted to see.

Jira information and assignee names may be confidential or personal information. Share exported reports only with authorised recipients and store or dispose of them in accordance with the applicable organisational requirements. Access to the app does not grant permission to redistribute Jira information publicly.

What the app does

The app reads Jira as the signed-in user and analyses the returned data in the browser. It does not create, update, assign, transition or delete Jira issues, record work logs or transfer budget funds. Its recovered pot is a calculated view of completed-ticket savings and overruns, not a separate fund or accounting ledger.

Budget settings change how allowances are interpreted in the view. Local settings are stored in the browser, not centrally shared with every user. Users should check the selected field mappings, units and contingency scenario when comparing results.

Recommendations and calculation limits

Outputs are advisory and depend on Jira data being accurate, complete and up to date. Refresh fetches a new snapshot; the app is not a continuously updating accounting system. Jira can omit information the viewer is not permitted to see, and reads across multiple requests are not a single transaction.

The budget scope is accessible direct non-subtask children of the selected epic. External linked work and subtasks are not included in those budget totals. Original estimate means Jira’s current field, not an immutable historical baseline. Budgets are shown as time, not money.

Empty or unavailable Complete and Used percentages display as 0%; check data notes and missing estimates before treating that figure as evidence that no work has occurred. Dependency recommendations reflect recorded links and workflow states, and they are not guaranteed schedules or delivery commitments.

Assignee reporting follows the current owner of each ticket. Logged time may have been recorded by other people. These reports should not be treated as a direct measure of individual performance or available capacity.

Verify the underlying records and assumptions before using app outputs for financial, staffing, contractual or delivery decisions. The app does not itself make those decisions.

Availability and support

Operation depends on Jira, Atlassian Forge, browser support, network availability and platform limits. The app may be unavailable, changed or withdrawn as required for maintenance, security or business needs. These draft terms do not create an uptime guarantee or a fixed support response time.

Report app problems and questions to devs@aerstudios.co.uk. Include the affected issue keys and a description where permitted, but do not send passwords, API tokens or unnecessary personal or confidential information.

Privacy and existing rights

Read the Privacy notice for details of the app’s data handling. Existing data ownership, confidentiality, employment, contractual and statutory rights continue to apply. Nothing in this document excludes any liability or right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Status and changes

This is a review draft, not an adopted agreement, and it has no effective date. AER should confirm that it fits the intended internal audience, existing agreements and applicable law before adoption. An adopted version should state its effective date and communicate material changes to affected users.
